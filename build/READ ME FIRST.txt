Axon Servo Programmer for macOS (unofficial)
============================================

You need the Axon Servo Programmer (the USB box) and an Axon servo.

1. Drag "Axon Servo Programmer" onto the Applications folder.
   (If you have an older version, replace it.)

2. The app isn't notarised by Apple. The first time, open Terminal and
   paste this one line:

   xattr -cr "/Applications/Axon Servo Programmer.app" && codesign --force --deep --sign - "/Applications/Axon Servo Programmer.app"

   (Or open it, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and
   click "Open Anyway".)

3. Open the app. Future versions update themselves from inside the app.

Project page: https://github.com/PetrBytes/axon-servo-programmer-mac

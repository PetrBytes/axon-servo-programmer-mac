Connect your servo

Plug the Axon Servo Programmer into your Mac (a USB-C adapter is fine) and connect a servo to it. Its settings load automatically.

You need the Axon Servo Programmer (the USB box the servo plugs into). This app can’t talk to a servo directly.

Servo Settings

Changes are only sent to the servo when you press Write.

Servo unplugged. Your unsaved changes are kept. Plug it back in to write them.

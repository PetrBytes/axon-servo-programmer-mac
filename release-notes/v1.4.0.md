## Axon Servo Programmer 1.4.0 (pre-release)

### Updates
- **Checks for updates every time the app opens**, then again while it stays open (every hour, 6 hours, day or week — default 6 hours). Previously a "daily" setting could miss a new release for up to a day.

### Storage
- **Clear Cache, Clear Log and Delete Backups now confirm first, then show the result** right on the button (e.g. "✓ Freed 12 MB") and refresh the sizes.
- **Automatic tidy-up at launch:** leftover update downloads and old app copies are deleted, and the cache is cleared automatically if it grows past 150 MB.

### Quitting
- **Press ⌘Q twice to quit**, like Chrome — a hint appears after the first press. Can be turned off in Settings → General. Quitting from the Dock still works straight away.

### Polish
- No more white flash when the app opens; the chosen theme is applied before the first frame.
- New slim, rounded scrollbar in the main area.
- Over Load Protection values are centred and aligned with their headers.
- Number boxes no longer show shifting spinner arrows.

### Install
Already on 1.2.0 or later? Open **Settings → Updates**.
New install: drag the app into Applications, then the first time either run

    xattr -cr "/Applications/Axon Servo Programmer.app" && codesign --force --deep --sign - "/Applications/Axon Servo Programmer.app"

or click **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Requires the Axon Servo Programmer (USB) and an Apple Silicon Mac.

⚠️ Pre-release, use at your own risk. Unofficial, not affiliated with Axon. Check servo values after writing and keep the Windows software as a backup.
